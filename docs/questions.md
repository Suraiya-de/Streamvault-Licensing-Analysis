# Open Questions and Assumptions

1. Are TMDb budget and revenue values complete and consistently reported across countries and release periods? The analysis treats them as reported values and does not adjust for inflation.
2. Do box-office gross and production budget adequately proxy licensing value? They omit streaming rights costs, distribution splits, marketing, residuals and contractual terms.
3. Should one-film collections be excluded, or could they represent a broader franchise opportunity outside the supplied candidate list? The primary analysis excludes them and the sensitivity analysis treats them as standalone.
4. Are all candidate titles and collection memberships representative of the content StreamVault can actually license? Availability and rights ownership require separate verification.
5. The sample is a supplied candidate list, not a random sample of all films; historical performance should not be interpreted as a forecast.
